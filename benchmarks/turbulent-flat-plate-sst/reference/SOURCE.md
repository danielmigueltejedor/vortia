# Reference

Prandtl–Schlichting skin-friction law for a smooth flat plate at zero pressure
gradient, turbulent from the leading edge:

    C_D = 0.455 / (log10 Re_L)^2.58      (about 1e6 < Re_L < 1e9)

Source: H. Schlichting, K. Gersten, *Boundary-Layer Theory*, 9th ed., Springer
(2017), chapter on the turbulent flat-plate boundary layer.

This is an empirical correlation fitted to measurements, with a scatter of a
few per cent. It is not an exact solution: agreement with it is a plausibility
check of the turbulence set-up, not a validation of Vortia against experiment.
No data are copied from the book; only the published formula is used.
